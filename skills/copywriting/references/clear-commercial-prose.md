# Clear commercial prose

Use this reference to improve the words inside an approved commercial structure. It adapts the Paul Graham writing synthesis to commercial copy without imitating his voice or turning a landing page into an essay.

## Quality function

Judge every claim on four dimensions:

| Dimension | Commercial standard |
| --- | --- |
| Important | Helps the intended buyer understand the outcome, mechanism, tradeoff, proof, or next step. |
| Novel | Says something product-specific that the reader may not already know. |
| Correct | Stays inside evidence and uses the narrowest qualification needed to remain true. |
| Strong | States the most consequential supported version of the claim, not the loudest version. |

Correctness is the floor. A false or unsupported line fails even when it is vivid.

## Revision loop

1. Map every line to the job it performs in the supplied formula or surface.
2. State the strongest supported claim in words you would use with a smart colleague.
3. Replace abstract benefits with an observable workflow, result, number, constraint, or decision.
4. Keep the reader's end-to-end outcome visible beyond the product's immediate interface.
5. Replace status language, ceremony, and sales adjectives with ordinary nouns and verbs.
6. Add a qualification only when scope, certainty, or integrity requires it.
7. Read the copy aloud and rewrite any phrase that sounds performed, tangled, or unlike speech.
8. Reread as a skeptical stranger with no access to the writer's intention or private context.
9. Cut lines that only repeat a heading, decorate the argument, flatter the audience, or restate another section.
10. Confirm that the final copy still performs every semantic job in the original formula.

Draft loosely enough to find the clearest idea, then revise tightly enough that only useful words survive. A long sentence is acceptable when every clause is required; simplicity is not uniform shortness.

## Commercial application

- Preserve formulas semantically, not mechanically. The same promise can be expressed more naturally without losing the formula's outcome, pain, mechanism, or next-step slot.
- Write for a smart, fair stranger. Audience awareness should force clarity and completeness, not flattery or invented consensus.
- Make the claim maximally strong, not maximally loud. Remove hedges when the evidence supports the broader claim; add exact limits when it does not.
- Use specialized terms when the intended reader uses them and they improve precision. Do not add jargon merely to sound expert.
- Let each section add one new idea. If the paragraph merely renames the headline, replace it with a mechanism, condition, consequence, or example.
- Keep implementation detail below the value it enables. A sales rep usually cares that a prospect is enriched and routed into a CRM or campaign before they care about the payload format.
- Use lists for true inventories, comparisons, steps, and checklists. Do not split one continuous argument into a list merely because lists scan well.
- Treat writing friction as product or positioning evidence. If the offer cannot be stated clearly, surface the ambiguity instead of hiding it with polished language.

## Examples

**Implementation-first**

> Review the JSON payload before sending the webhook.

**Outcome-first**

> Capture the prospect while you browse, then send it through Clay for enrichment and routing.

The revision keeps the mechanism available elsewhere but leads with the sales workflow it enables.

**Vague role for the user**

> Wedge handles the repeated fields; you add the judgment.

**Concrete role for the user**

> Wedge fills the repeated fields; you add any important comments.

The revision names the actual action instead of asking the reader to interpret an abstraction.

**Generic call to action**

> Install Codebound

**Specific call to action**

> Build my GTM stack

The revision names the result of the next step.

**Ceremonial claim**

> Wedge revolutionizes your research workflow with a seamless, powerful integration.

**Specific claim**

> Capture a company or contact while you browse. Get it enriched and routed through Clay in seconds.

The revision uses ordinary words, names an observable action, and makes a claim that can be checked.

## Admission (skill-issue steps 1–3)

- Survivor categories: chosen-among-equals conventions (state layout, the living prototype and its gap
  markers, manifest, question and handoff text, copy format, page design); taste and quality bars (decision
  fidelity, the review's severities and bar, "the prototype is the spec"); authority structure (client-owned
  decisions, bar and rounds, risk acceptance, "That's it"); deterministic scripts and assets (`serve.sh`,
  `check-step.sh`, the shell).
- Type: Process SOP. Name: gerund `consulting`.
- Reduction: "When a client asks for a consultation on an ambiguous change, the Consultant runs the
  one-question interview on one living prototype of the whole thing, the gap review, the final check and the
  handoff, using the supplied territory and durable state, to produce a frozen prototype that is the
  builder's spec, a plan of only what it cannot show, and a list of what is left open, while never
  implementing, never editing the client's project and never asking for a lookup-able fact."
- The description canon's non-overlap rule is knowingly waived against `consultant` (client decision 10 of
  the first version: `consulting` is the long-term owner; `consultant` is retired later).

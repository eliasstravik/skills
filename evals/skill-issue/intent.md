# skill-issue — rewrite intent

## Outcome

Replace the discarded primitive/core/Details standard with a from-scratch Policy/standards SOP that treats every agent skill as one human SOP.

## Contract

- Preserve the name `skill-issue` and make it model-invoked.
- Admit only content in six durable survivor categories; reject competence-only proposals.
- Apply the perfect-reasoning plus perfect-public-knowledge litmus to every body line.
- Classify each admitted skill as Artifact, Process, Lifecycle, Task, or Policy/standards and name it by type.
- Require Trigger, Scope, Inputs, Roles, Procedure, Outputs, Exceptions, QC, and References in that order.
- Overflow bulky non-derivable material to `references/` and deterministic operations to `scripts/`.
- Enforce vendor ceilings rather than a house body cap.
- Let the frozen-query optimizer own the final description verbatim.

## Evaluation contract

Five fresh paired cases cover admission rejection, artifact/process naming, lifecycle naming, nine-section authoring, and references/scripts overflow. Pure baselines forbid every installed skill; treatments load only the run-local candidate. Every arm stays in its run directory and saves a transcript, timing, complete events, outputs, and programmatic evidence.

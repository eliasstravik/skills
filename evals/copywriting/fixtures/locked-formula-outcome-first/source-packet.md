# Scoutlane landing-page copy packet

## Product truth

Scoutlane is a Chrome extension for sales representatives who prospect from company websites and professional profiles. One click captures the company or contact plus the current page context and sends it to an existing Clay webhook. Clay can enrich the record and route it into the team's CRM or outbound campaign. Before sending, the rep may add an optional comment.

Scoutlane does not perform enrichment itself. It does not include a CRM, sequencing, or campaign engine. It is free and MIT licensed. No customer count, testimonial, speed benchmark, or adoption claim is approved.

## Audience and moment

The reader is a sales representative moving quickly from one prospect to the next. Copying the same fields into several systems breaks their browsing flow and creates a backlog they must process later.

## Change

The Switch remains the single core primitive. Five new Details lines call one
shipping reference and encode the recurring failures from live editing:

- preserve supplied formulas semantically rather than replacing them or copying awkward wording;
- lead with the reader's downstream workflow before implementation detail;
- prefer the shortest headline that still makes the outcome clear;
- use specific next-result or real product-action CTAs;
- revise finished copy with the important + novel + correct + strong quality function, ordinary speech, skeptical-stranger rereading, read-aloud testing, and aggressive cuts.

The shipping reference includes approved transformations from implementation-first to outcome-first copy, abstract user labor to a concrete action, generic install copy to a result-led CTA, and ceremonial marketing language to a testable product claim.
